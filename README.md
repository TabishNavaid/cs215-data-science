## Final project ideas

### 1. Downtown Walla Walla storefronts

Collect data on which downtown storefronts are open, empty, or turning over, and look for patterns by street, rent, and type of business.

### 2. AI policies in course syllabi

Use a dataset of generative AI policies from intro course syllabi to see how strict or open schools are, and whether that changes by type of school or over time.

### 3. When internship postings open

Gather posting dates for tech internships across companies and years, then try to predict when a given company will open its applications.
